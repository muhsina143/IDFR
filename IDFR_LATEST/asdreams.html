How we work?

From the first mood board to the final colour grade, one dedicated team plans, shoots and edits every story — so nothing gets lost between the shutter and the screen.

Behind the Lens, Ahead of the Curve

ASDreamStudio has photographed and filmed every IDFR season since the very first runway. We pair editorial instinct with cinema-grade gear to turn fleeting moments — a designer's final bow, a first look, a sunrise vow — into images that last a lifetime.

  • Runway, backstage and campaign coverage for every IDFR season
  • Cinema cameras, drones and a fully equipped lighting studio
  • Same-week edits, colour grading and print-ready albums

Our Kozhikode studio houses a 2,400 sq ft cyclorama, a dedicated edit suite and a styling lounge. Beyond IDFR, we travel across India and abroad for weddings, destination shoots, brand films and live events — always with the same small, senior crew.
