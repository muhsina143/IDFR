01 — About IDFR

The IDFR Story

Fashion is a stage. Every talent deserves its spotlight.

IDFR — Indian Designer Fashion Runway — was founded to bridge the gap between modelling talent and the opportunities that shape careers. Whether you are a fresh face taking your very first step or an experienced professional refining your portfolio, IDFR gives you a stage to be seen.

Through our seasonal fashion shows, verified digital profiles and a growing network of casting recruiters, brands, photographers and designers, models on IDFR receive assignments from hundreds of industry professionals across India.

Season after season, we continue to build a community where creativity, confidence and opportunity walk the same runway.
